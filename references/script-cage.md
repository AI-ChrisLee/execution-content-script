## THE CAGE (what all six share)

1. **HOOK, 15-60s.** Claim with a specific number → proof ON SCREEN before any setup
   (montage, dashboard, the finished artifact) → the roadmap SPOKEN in one compressed
   breath (numbered or run-on; never a slide TOC) → the credibility line, one breath,
   placed right after the promise (Kallaway's "by the way" aside; Hormozi's numbers
   ARE the open) → one or two open-loop teases ("stick around for the gate where...")
   → hard cut into content.
2. **THE CASE, 0-25% and optional.** NOT a mourning problem-agitate block. The winners
   either skip it entirely (Kallaway, Hormozi), fold the pain INTO the hook as a
   scenario (zapiwala's 2am scroll), or spend it killing the one objection / making
   the why-now case (Metics vs Wix; Kellan vs "saturated"). Short, positive-frame,
   ends on a re-hook pivot line.
3. **INSTALL THE RUBRIC.** Metics's retention spine: a checklist stated early, then
   re-graded on camera mid-run ("four boxes checked, four to go") and closed at the
   end. The scorecard is the progress bar.
4. **THE STACK, 53-99% (the main part).** Never one long arc: numbered beats, each
   ending in a VISIBLE artifact, each with its own mini-payoff (Blackman's loops,
   60-90s apart). Inside it: a stakes-flag line right before the key moment ("the
   most important moment in the whole run"), ONE pattern-break where the system
   pushes back or refuses (Metics's React refusal; plants a detail paid off later),
   skip-ahead compression on every wait, and the MID CTA placed immediately AFTER the
   first marquee reveal, at peak curiosity (Starter Story 4:26), phrased as a
   resource, not an ask.
5. **CLOSE, 0.3-17%, short and abrupt.** Final re-grade closes the rubric loop, a
   FRESH bookend proof (a new screenshot, the total-cost math), the payoff restated
   against the hook's promise, then one soft ask maximum plus a curiosity gap into
   the next video. Never signal the ending early, never trail off.

CTA law across the set: resource links tied to the exact on-screen step are unlimited
and read as service; channel asks are at most one, at the close (the two biggest
videos here have zero or one).

# execution-content-script

This agent is a base. Once you have done it your way, tell your squad "update the agent to do it like this."

Agent 8. It writes your YouTube script, `03_SCRIPT.md`, in your episode folder. First it writes the
prep, `00_PREP.md`: the winning video's structure row by row with what yours does in each, the beats
with their key lines word for word, the real questions from the winner's comments that open each
section, what every tool costs read off each tool's own page, your 1 real moment, and the length. You
read the prep and fix a word. Then it writes the script: every line you say, every screen you open,
every line the viewer copies held on screen, the chapters timed off your own words a minute.

**Install.** Installed with the one line on aichrislee.com/free, then quit and reopen Claude Code.

**What it reads.** The package execution-content-scrape locked in `squad/week/`, and the winner file that package names, its structure table first. Your offer page, `squad/business.md`, says who the video is for. The real messages you gave your squad on day 1 are the voice.

**Run it.** Type "Write the money script." It asks 2 things: 1 real moment when something went wrong, broke or surprised you, and what on your screen shows the thing you sell working. Read the prep, fix a word, then type "The prep is approved." The script lands in the same folder.

It never records, posts or publishes. The recording is yours.
